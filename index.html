Building AI's Data Core: Architecting Scalable Data Foundations for AI

The bottleneck in AI has shifted downstream. Data structure, access, and orchestration across complex environments now determine what can scale.

As AI systems expand in scope and complexity, gaps in data flow, inconsistent access patterns, and brittle pipeline design are becoming critical failure points. These issues surface most clearly under production workloads, where latency, reliability, and context continuity are non-negotiable.

This roundtable webinar explores how technical leaders are addressing these constraints by engineering a scalable foundation built to support modern AI workloads in production environments.

You'll Learn How To:

  • Spot where AI systems break at scale in data flow, access, and latency
  • Understand why traditional pipelines fail for generative and agentic systems
  • Define what production AI architectures require
  • Improve orchestration and reliability at the system level
