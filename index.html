Data Governance That Sticks: Embedding
Trust in the Work Your Business Already Does

Many data governance programs fail when they ask the business to adopt a new process on top of the work it's already doing. The result is friction, bottlenecks, and governance that lives in meetings instead of in the data itself.

Join Heather Feist, Data Governance Lead at Gray to hear how she turned governance from a bolt-on initiative into a durable capability that scaled across a complex, multi-subsidiary business without slowing anyone down. You'll hear how Gray built a new model of governance around roadmaps, guardrails, and signals, used trusted ambassadors in business over technical specialists, and made Informatica's data catalog the system of record for ownership, lineage, and trust — laying the foundation for AI-ready data along the way.

In this session, you'll learn how to:

  • Embed governance into initiatives the business already cares about
  • Use roadmaps, guardrails, and signals to scale governance without chasing perfection
  • Turn governance from a compliance exercise into a measurable, AI-ready capability using an enterprise data catalog
